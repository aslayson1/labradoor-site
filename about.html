About Labradoor

Labradoor is a camera-first property-intelligence product. Snap a home to identify the property and retrieve hidden property details, then investigate ownership, estimated equity, mortgages, seller signals, and investment scenarios.

One product, three ways to research

The iPhone app supports research in the field. The web platform provides room for Prospect searches and analysis. The Chrome extension adds context alongside properties you are already viewing online.

What our guides explain

Labradoor publishes product and property-research guides. Recorded facts, estimates, and research signals have different levels of certainty. A Motivation Score does not predict a sale. Mortgage indicators do not confirm assumption eligibility. Numerical scenarios are examples, not financing offers or guaranteed returns.

Articles show the publication date and author where provided. References support the topic discussed; product screenshots illustrate the relevant workflow. Verify important financial or property details with the current source before acting.
