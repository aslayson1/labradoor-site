Test the assumptions behind a property with Labradoor Analyzer

Move from property research to rental, flip, and short-term rental scenarios with assumptions you can inspect.

An interesting property still has to pass the math

Labradoor Analyzer helps evaluate property scenarios rather than stopping at a listing price or an estimate. The product supports rental, flip, and short-term rental analysis. Adjust supported assumptions to understand how the result changes.

A verdict is only as useful as the inputs behind it. Check the purchase price, financing, income, operating costs, repair assumptions, and time horizon relevant to the strategy you are evaluating.

Run a realistic case and a downside case

Start with evidence you can explain. Use current comparable information for the property and market where it is available. Then evaluate what happens if income is lower, repairs cost more, or the project takes longer than expected.

Compare the supported return measures in context, such as cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, or yield. Different measures answer different questions; a strong result on one measure does not remove financing or operating risk.

Verify before you commit

Estimated property value, mortgage information, and projected income are research inputs. Confirm the information with current records, appropriate professionals, and property-specific due diligence before making an offer or investment decision.

Use Prospect to discover properties and ownership or mortgage context to ask better questions. Use the Analyzer to make your assumptions visible. None of those steps guarantees a return.
