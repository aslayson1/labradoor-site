1. Scope

The Services are intended for users located in the United States. We do not knowingly offer the Services to, or target, individuals in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, and this Privacy Policy is not intended to describe rights under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation.

If you access the Services from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

A. Information You Provide to Us

When you use Labradoor, you may provide:

  • Your name, email address, and password when creating an account
  • Login information when signing in with Google or Apple
  • Property search inputs, including addresses and other property-related information
  • Saved properties and favorites tied to your account
  • Communications you send to us, including support requests, account deletion requests, and feedback

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Services, we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • General usage activity within the app
  • Error logs and diagnostic information

C. Precise Geolocation Information (Sensitive Personal Information)

Precise geolocation is treated as "sensitive personal information" or "sensitive data" under the privacy laws of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other states. We collect it only with your affirmative, opt-in consent.

With your permission, Labradoor collects your device location to help confirm the correct property address when you photograph a home. We use precise geolocation solely for that purpose — to identify and confirm the property you are looking at — and for no other purpose.

We do not use precise geolocation to infer characteristics about you, to build advertising or marketing profiles, for targeted advertising, or for any purpose unrelated to identifying the property you are viewing. We do not sell or share precise geolocation.

Withdrawing consent: You can revoke location access at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Labradoor). Revoking consent stops all future collection. Certain camera-based property identification features may not work accurately without location access.

D. Camera Access

With your permission, Labradoor may access your camera so you can photograph homes. Labradoor may use the image, along with the address number, physical location, orientation, or a combination of those signals, to help determine the correct property address.

We only access the camera when you choose to use camera-based features. You can revoke camera access at any time in your device settings.

We do not use photographs to identify people. See Section 13 (Biometric Information).

E. Photo Library Access

With your permission, Labradoor may access your photo library so you can select and upload a profile picture. We only access your photo library when you choose to update your profile picture, and only the photo you select is uploaded — we do not scan or access the rest of your photo library. You can revoke photo library access at any time in your device settings.

F. Microphone and Voice Search

With your permission, Labradoor may access your microphone so you can speak a property address instead of typing it. Audio you speak is processed using Apple's on-device and server-based Speech Recognition service to convert your speech to text; this processing is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not otherwise record, store, or retain your voice audio, and we do not use voice data to identify you as an individual (no voiceprint or voice biometric identification is performed — see Section 13).

We only access the microphone when you choose to use voice search. You can revoke microphone and speech recognition access at any time in your device settings.

G. Authentication and Account Data

If you sign in using Google or Apple, we receive account authentication information necessary to sign you in and maintain your account.

H. Subscription and Purchase Information

If you purchase a paid subscription, we may receive information related to your subscription status, entitlement level, and transaction metadata necessary to provide premium access. Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other financial account credentials.

G. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and do not ask you to provide:

  • Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, passport numbers, or other government identifiers
  • Financial account numbers or payment card numbers
  • Biometric identifiers or biometric information
  • Health, medical, or genetic information
  • Information about your racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, union membership, immigration status, or citizenship status
  • The contents of your mail, email, or text messages

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the rights described below. We extend the core rights in this section to all U.S. users, regardless of state of residence.

These rights are provided under laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and the comprehensive consumer privacy laws of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, and Virginia, as well as other states as their laws take effect.

  • Right to know / access. Request confirmation of whether we process your personal information, and access to the specific pieces and categories we hold, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties to whom we disclose it.
  • Right to delete. Request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct. Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to data portability. Obtain a copy of your personal information in a portable and, to the extent technically feasible, readily usable format.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising, and profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. As described in Section 7, we do not engage in any of these activities.
  • Right to limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information, including precise geolocation. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Right to withdraw consent to processing of sensitive data, including precise geolocation and camera access, at any time through your device settings.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny you goods or services, charge you a different price, or provide a different level or quality of service because you exercised a privacy right. We do not offer financial incentives in exchange for personal information.
  • Right to appeal. If we decline your request, you may appeal — see Section 9.
  • Right to opt out of automated decision-making. We do not use your personal information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

Minnesota, Oregon, and certain other states additionally give you the right to obtain a list of the specific third parties (not merely categories) to whom we have disclosed personal information. You may request this list using the contact methods in Section 9.

9. How to Exercise Your Rights

To submit a privacy request, use either method below:

  • Email: privacy@labradoor.ai (or hello@labradoor.ai) with the subject line "Privacy Request"
  • In the app: account deletion is available directly in your account settings

Please tell us which right you wish to exercise and the email address associated with your account.

Verification. To protect your information, we will verify your identity before acting on a request — typically by confirming control of the email address associated with your account. We may request additional information if we cannot otherwise verify you. We will not use verification information for any other purpose.

Authorized agents. You may designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf. We may require the agent to provide proof of authorization, and may require you to verify your own identity directly with us.

Response timing. We will acknowledge receipt within 10 business days where required, and respond substantively within 45 days. We may extend that period by an additional 45 days (90 days in some states) where reasonably necessary, and will notify you of the extension and the reason. Requests are free unless manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive, in which case we may charge a reasonable fee or decline, and will explain why.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or emailing privacy@labradoor.ai with the subject line "Privacy Appeal." We will respond in writing within 45 days (60 days in some states), explaining the reasons for our decision. If we deny your appeal, we will provide you with a method to contact your state Attorney General to submit a complaint.
